Three guys, three chairs, a lap full of slide guitar, and no drummer required. A rare night where all three sat down together and played as one — and bands 2, 3 and 4 of my 49 ticked off in one go.

#2
JOHN BUTLER
On the left · Fremantle, WA

Born in California, raised in Western Australia, and got his start busking on the streets of Fremantle. That busking act grew into the John Butler Trio, one of the biggest independent bands Australia has produced — Sunrise Over Sea and Grand National both went to No. 1.

Known for a wild, open-tuned 11- and 12-string guitar style. His instrumental “Ocean” is the one to watch — one guy, one guitar, no hurry.

#3
BEN HARPER
In the middle · Claremont, California

That thing across his knees is a Weissenborn lap-slide guitar — he grew up in his family's music shop in Claremont and made it his signature sound. Blues, soul, folk, reggae and rock, all at once.

Three-time Grammy winner, huge in Australia and France, and the reason a lot of people first heard slide guitar played like that. Start with “Diamonds on the Inside”.

“Diamonds on the Inside” · Watch on YouTube ↗
#4
JACK JOHNSON
On the right · North Shore, Oahu, Hawaii

Was on track to be a pro surfer until a bad wipeout at Pipeline as a teenager. Went on to make surf films, played a few songs on the soundtracks, and accidentally became one of the biggest-selling acoustic artists of the 2000s.

Laid-back, barefoot, beach-y — if you've been in a café since 2005 you've heard him. Try “Better Together”; you'll know it.
